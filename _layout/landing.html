Article counts by publication year

This query counts records classified as articles for 2015–2025. Its unit of analysis is the work record. Changes in counts can reflect source coverage and metadata updates as well as publication activity.

research_question.sql

How many article records occur in each publication year?

SELECT publication_year,
       COUNT(*) AS works
FROM openalex.works
WHERE publication_year BETWEEN 2015 AND 2025
  AND type = 'article'
GROUP BY publication_year
ORDER BY publication_year;
Illustrative SQL; no result is computed here. Counts depend on the loaded snapshot.

Publication-date quality audits

The repository contains frozen stratified sampling, metadata comparisons, full-text retrieval and review, and reports assessing whether dates support temporal citation analysis. The initial design samples 1,000 works in each of eight publication-year strata; DOI-only repeats define a separate target population.

A completed 14 September 2026 report reviews 800 archived PDFs, 100 per decade from 1950–1959 through 2020–2026, drawn from DOI-bearing works labeled English articles. It separates online publication, issue/volume evidence, partial dates, and unresolved events. The findings do not validate every OpenAlex date as the day of first public availability.

For nine sampled works, the audit retrieved linked arXiv submission histories and recorded journal DOI agreement. This is bounded evidence gathering for the date audit; a general OpenAlex–arXiv crosswalk remains planned in Data matching.

Define the analytical population

The snapshot is not a complete census of research. Specify work types, dates, and source coverage; inspect missing metadata and account for multiple authors, affiliations, and topics when joining tables.
