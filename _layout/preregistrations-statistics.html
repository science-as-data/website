AsPredicted

25,436 stored observations represent 25,378 distinct registration IDs. The 58 additional rows preserve repeated retrieval observations. 25,378 IDs have a nonblank hypothesis; 25,376 have planned analyses; 25,374 have a sample-size field.

Field presence means a nonblank value in at least one stored observation; it does not establish the plan’s completeness or quality. Registration and creation dates are kept distinct from retrieval dates and study start dates.
